The Reading Ritual

A reading practice that actually sticks

Most reading advice is all-or-nothing. Read 50 books a year. Take detailed notes on everything. It burns people out before it builds anything.

Recall is built around a different idea: small, consistent rituals that compound. Five focused minutes of reflection does more for retention than hours of passive reading.

1
Clear your head

Two minutes to quiet the noise. Read with intention, not obligation.

2
Read at your pace

A chapter, ten pages, twenty minutes - whatever fits your day. Consistency beats volume.

3
Reflect with Recall

A few minutes to talk through what you read. Ask questions. Make connections. Let the ideas land.
