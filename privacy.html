Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 8, 2026

Recall is built on a simple idea: your reading life is personal. We collect only what we need to make the app work and we don't sell your data to third parties.

1. Who we are

Recall is a mobile reading app ("we," "us," or "our"). You can reach us at hello@readingrecall.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in two ways:

Information you give us directly:

  • Your email address, when you sign up for the waitlist or create an account
  • Books you add, notes you write, and reflections you save inside the app
  • Any feedback or messages you send us

Information collected automatically:

  • Basic usage data (which features you use, session duration) to help us improve the app
  • Device type and operating system version for debugging and compatibility
  • Crash reports if the app encounters an error

We do not collect your location, contacts, camera, or microphone unless a specific feature requires it and you explicitly grant permission.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide and maintain the Recall app and its features
  • To send you waitlist updates, product announcements, and occasional newsletters (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • To understand how people use Recall so we can improve it
  • To respond to your questions and support requests
  • To improve Recall's AI features - including cross-book context (connecting themes and ideas across your library) and personalized recommendations - using anonymized, aggregated data
  • The Recall conversation feature is powered by third-party AI models. Content you share in a conversation - such as your thoughts on a book - may be processed by these providers to generate a response. We use a combination of AI models and may update which providers we use over time. In future versions of Recall, conversation data may be used to improve your personal experience across books, such as building context about your reading history and interests. We will notify you before this feature is enabled and give you the choice to opt in.
  • To display in-app advertising in the future. If and when we show ads, they may be informed by your in-app activity - including reading categories, notes, and reflections - to make them more relevant. We do not sell this data to third parties or use it to build a profile of you outside of Recall
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. How we store your information

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We retain your account data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services.

To request account deletion, email us at hello@readingrecall.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days, except where we are required to retain data for legal reasons.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share it only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: We use trusted third-party services to help operate Recall (such as Supabase for database hosting, Apple for app distribution, and AI model providers including OpenAI, Anthropic, and others for powering the Recall conversation feature). These providers access only what they need to perform their services and are contractually required to protect your data.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Recall or others.
  • Business transfers: If Recall is acquired or merges with another company, your data may be transferred. We will notify you before this happens.

6. Your rights and choices

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Update your account information (such as your email address) directly within the app
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@readingrecall.com.

7. Children's privacy

Recall is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Third-party links and services

The app may contain links to third-party websites or services (such as book retailers or Wikipedia). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or through a notice in the app. The "last updated" date at the top reflects when changes were most recently made. Continued use of Recall after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
